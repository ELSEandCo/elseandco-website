Co-Founder

Irely Fajardo

Experiences, Business Development & Partnerships

With a background in Hospitality Management, postgraduate studies in project management, and over 15 years of international experience in sales, business development, and hospitality, Irely brings a people-oriented and experience-driven perspective to ELSE & CO.

Her professional journey combines Caribbean roots with international business experience across hospitality and telecommunications. Her expertise includes client relationships, commercial strategy, business development, and creating meaningful connections.

Naturally curious and creative, Irely believes exceptional experiences are built around people, thoughtful details, and a genuine sense of discovery.

At ELSE & CO., she brings the vision, connections, and hospitality mindset that transform ideas into meaningful journeys and memorable experiences.

Co-Founder

Karla Gomez

Strategy, Finance & Operations

With a background in Economics, an MBA, and extensive experience in finance, business operations, and strategic planning, Karla brings structure and clarity to ambitious ideas.

Her professional journey spans the Dominican Republic, Sweden, and the United States, bringing together Caribbean roots, international business experience, and a multicultural perspective. Her expertise includes financial management, budgeting, risk assessment, and operational coordination.

Analytical by nature and practical by experience, Karla believes that exceptional experiences require more than creativity - they need thoughtful planning, smart decisions, and seamless execution.

At ELSE & CO., she brings the strategic and operational perspective that transforms beautiful ideas into well-executed realities.
