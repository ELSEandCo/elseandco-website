Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Do you work in any destination?

We work internationally, subject to destination feasibility, supplier availability and the scope agreed in your proposal.

Are vendor and venue costs included?

No. Our professional fee covers the planning and coordination scope. Venues, travel, accommodation, suppliers and other external costs are separate.

Can you coordinate an event we planned ourselves?

Yes. Coordination at the Destination is designed for clients who already have the main plan and suppliers in place but need professional management when the event takes place.

Do you plan only weddings?

No. Weddings are a flagship service, but we also design retreats, celebrations, incentive trips and private or corporate group experiences.
