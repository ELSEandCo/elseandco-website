Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Who is The Edit for?

We work with hotels, villas, resorts, retreats and other travel or hospitality businesses that want stronger operations, guest experiences or commercial processes.

Where should we begin?

If the challenge is not yet clearly defined, the Hospitality Audit is the best starting point. It creates a prioritized view before a larger engagement is proposed.

Do you implement the recommendations?

Implementation support can be included in a project or continued through Ongoing Advisory. The exact responsibilities and deliverables are confirmed in the proposal.

Are you an AI consultancy?

No. We are hospitality consultants who use technology and AI where it can reduce friction, improve communication and save time without weakening the human experience.
