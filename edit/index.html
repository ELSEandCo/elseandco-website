Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Who is The Edit for?

We work with travel, hospitality and experience businesses that want stronger operations, customer journeys, sales or internal processes.

Where should we begin?

If the challenge is not yet clearly defined, the Business Audit is the best starting point. It creates a prioritized view before a larger engagement is proposed.

Do you implement the recommendations?

Implementation support can be included in a project or continued through Ongoing Advisory. The exact responsibilities and deliverables are confirmed in the proposal.

Are you an AI consultancy?

No. We are business consultants who use technology and AI when it can reduce friction, improve communication and save time without weakening the human experience.
